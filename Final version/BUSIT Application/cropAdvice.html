Part 3: Implementing Modern Technology

Drones:
  • Used for aerial seeding across large fields.
  • Monitor crop health and soil conditions using sensors.
  • Apply fertilizers and pesticides precisely.
Virtual Reality (VR):
  • Simulates field training and planting practices.
  • Helps farmers visualize crop growth and manage risks.
3D Printing:
  • Produces custom seed planting tools and equipment parts.
  • Used in educational models to teach seed structure.
  • Enables quick prototyping of new planting devices.

Transport and Planting Process

Transport:
  • Seeds are packaged in airtight, moisture-proof bags.
  • Labels include seed type, batch number, and production date.
  • Stored in cool, dry warehouses

Part 1: Five Basic Agricultural Seed Types

Cereal Seeds
  • Examples: Maize, wheat, rice
  • Purpose: Staple food crops for grain production.
Legume Seeds
  • Examples: Beans, peas, lentils
  • Purpose: Protein-rich and improve soil through nitrogen fixation.
Vegetable Seeds
  • Examples: Tomato, spinach, carrots
  • Purpose: Grown for fresh produce and nutritional variety.
Oilseed Crops
  • Examples: Sunflower, canola, soybeans
  • Purpose: Used for cooking oil and livestock feed.
Forage Seeds
  • Examples: Alfalfa, clover, ryegrass
  • Purpose: Planted for animal feed and pasture.
